## 🛠 Data Cleaning Steps

### ✅ 1. Duplicate Validation

Verified that every customer had a unique **Customer_ID** and confirmed that duplicate records were not present.

```sql
SELECT Customer_ID,
COUNT(*) AS Duplicate_Count
FROM churn
GROUP BY Customer_ID
HAVING COUNT(*) > 1;
```

---

### ✅ 2. Missing Value Analysis

Validated important business columns for NULL and blank values.

```sql
SELECT *
FROM churn
WHERE Customer_Status IS NULL
OR TRIM(Customer_Status)='';
```

---

### ✅ 3. Data Type Validation

Standardized numeric and categorical columns to ensure compatibility across SQL queries, Power BI, and Machine Learning.

Examples:

* Monthly Charges → Decimal
* Total Revenue → Decimal
* Age → Integer
* Tenure → Integer
* Customer Status → Text

---

| Monthly Charge Range | Pricing analysis      |

---

### ✅ 5. Final Validation

Performed final quality checks before analysis.


## 🔍 Example SQL Queries

### Customer Distribution

```sql
SELECT Customer_Status,
COUNT(*) AS Total_Customers
FROM churn
GROUP BY Customer_Status;
```

---

### Churn by Contract Type

```sql
SELECT Contract,
COUNT(*) AS Churned_Customers
FROM churn
WHERE Customer_Status='Churned'
GROUP BY Contract
ORDER BY Churned_Customers DESC;
```

---

### Average Monthly Charges

```sql
SELECT Customer_Status,
ROUND(AVG(Monthly_Charge),2)
AS Average_Charge
FROM churn
GROUP BY Customer_Status;
```

---

### Top Revenue Customers

```sql
SELECT Customer_ID,
Total_Revenue
FROM churn
ORDER BY Total_Revenue DESC
LIMIT 10;
```

# 📈 Business Insights

The analysis uncovered several patterns that directly influence customer churn and business performance. These insights were derived through **SQL analysis**, **Python EDA**, **Machine Learning**, and **Power BI dashboards**.

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1️⃣ Month-to-Month Contracts Have the Highest Churn

Customers subscribed to **Month-to-Month** contracts exhibit the highest churn rate compared to customers on long-term contracts.

**Business Impact**

* Higher customer acquisition costs
* Reduced customer lifetime value
* Increased revenue loss

---

### 2️⃣ Short-Tenure Customers Are More Likely to Leave

Customers with fewer months of service are significantly more likely to churn.

**Business Impact**

* Indicates weak onboarding
* Highlights need for early engagement programs

---

### 3️⃣ Higher Monthly Charges Increase Churn Risk

Customers paying higher monthly fees are more likely to switch providers.

**Business Impact**

* Pricing strategy directly influences customer retention.

---

### 4️⃣ Contract Type Is the Strongest Predictor

The type of contract plays a greater role in churn than demographic attributes.

---

### 5️⃣ Customers Without Tech Support Churn More Frequently

Customers who do not subscribe to Tech Support services have noticeably higher churn rates.

---

### 6️⃣ Internet Service Type Influences Retention

Certain internet service plans experience higher customer attrition, indicating service quality and customer experience impact loyalty.

---

### 7️⃣ Payment Method Affects Customer Behavior

Different payment methods are associated with varying churn patterns.

---

### 8️⃣ Customer Demographics Alone Do Not Explain Churn

Business-related variables such as contract, tenure, billing, and services have a greater influence than age or gender.

---

### 9️⃣ Machine Learning Successfully Identified High-Risk Customers

The Random Forest model predicts customers likely to churn before they leave.

---

### 🔟 Interactive Dashboards Improve Decision-Making

Power BI dashboards provide real-time insights into churn trends, enabling stakeholders to make faster and more informed decisions.
